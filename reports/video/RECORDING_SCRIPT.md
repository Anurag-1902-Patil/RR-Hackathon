# SupeRR Intelligence: pitch recording script

Read this as if you're talking to Kumar Sangakkara across a table, not presenting to a hall. Smile while you speak; it carries in your voice.
Total is about 2 minutes 45 seconds at a relaxed pace.

## How to record (2 minutes of setup, saves you re-takes)

1. **One file per slide**, named `slide01`, `slide02` … `slide10`. Any format works: .wav, .mp3 or .m4a. A phone voice-memo app is fine.
2. Put the files in this folder: `reports/video/your_voice/`, then tell me.
3. Quiet room, phone or mic about 20 cm (a hand-span) from your mouth, slightly to the side so you don't "pop" on P and B sounds.
4. Leave a one-second pause before you start and after you finish each file. I trim silence and clean background hiss automatically.
5. If you stumble, don't stop: pause, then repeat the whole sentence. Or just re-record that one slide.
6. Words in **bold** are the ones to lean on. `/` means a short breath. `//` means a longer pause.

## slide01: Who delivers when it matters?  (about 12 s)

Every franchise knows the stars. /
The harder question / the one that actually wins auctions / is this: //
**who still delivers** when the game is on the line? /
And which of those players can Rajasthan Royals **actually sign**?

## slide02: The answer, up front  (about 22 s)

So here's our answer, up front. //
**Daryl Mitchell** leads the batters RR can sign. /
**Sikandar Raza** leads the bowlers / and he's fifth among the batters too. /
That makes Raza our **first target**. //
And from India's domestic T20, / **Rahul Buddhi** and **Narayan Jagadeesan** are the uncapped batters to watch.

## slide03: Pressure, defined before every ball  (about 20 s)

How do you measure pressure? //
Before every single ball, we ask two questions. /
**How much** could this ball swing the match? /
And **how hard** is the situation: the scoreboard, the wickets, the dot balls piling up? //
The toughest twenty percent of balls count as high pressure. / And yes, batters really do get out more often there.

## slide04: Fair comparison, honest uncertainty  (about 22 s)

Then we make it fair. /
Every ball is compared with what an average player would do / in the same spot, / against the same opponents. //
And here's the surprise: / **most of what we call clutch is noise.** /
Only about one-fifth of it is real. /
So we never reward a lucky streak, / and a hundred resamples tell us how sure we are about every name.

## slide05: The stars are all taken  (about 16 s)

Then one reality check changed everything. //
The very best under pressure: / Shreyas Iyer, Heinrich Klaasen, Varun Chakravarthy: / **they're all under contract.** /
A list of stars doesn't help on auction day. //
So we rank **only** the players RR can actually sign.

## slide06: Batters RR can sign  (about 14 s)

With the bat: / Mitchell, Campher, Mendis and Chapman stand out. /
And Raza sits in the **ninety-seventh percentile at the death**, /
exactly where Rajasthan have struggled most.

## slide07: Bowlers RR can sign  (about 13 s)

With the ball: / Raza, **Theekshana** and **Zampa** are near-certain picks, / top ten in almost every resample. //
And Theekshana already knows the Royals' dressing room.

## slide08: Uncapped, on Indian pitches  (about 22 s)

Then we went further. /
The data pack has no Indian domestic cricket, / so we added **two hundred and seventy-two** Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy matches, / played on Indian pitches. //
Buddhi and Jagadeesan lead the uncapped batters. /
Domestic batting form carries over to the IPL. / Bowling form doesn't, / and we say so honestly.

## slide09: The gap is at the death  (about 20 s)

Now, map it to Rajasthan. /
The top order is the **best in the league.** / The death overs / are not. //
Raza fixes the finish, / Theekshana and Zampa defend it, / Chapman or Mitchell steady the middle, /
and the uncapped batters fill the Indian slots.

## slide10: The auction plan  (about 18 s)

That's our auction plan. //
Built on every ball since twenty twenty-two, / honest about what we don't know, /
and ready to re-run the moment the retention lists drop. //
Who delivers when it matters? / *(pause, smile)* / **Now RR knows.**
